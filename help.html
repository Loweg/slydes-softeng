SLYDES GUIDE

This button is used to place a block of text into your presentation. Click it and type out what you want on your slide!

This button is used to insert an image into insert an image into your presentation. Clicking it will allow you to choose an image from your computer to place on your current slide!

This button is used to insert a hyperlink. Clicking it will allow you to insert a button which can be used to open a webpage during your presentation!

These buttons are used to bold, italicize, underline and change the size of text. Use these to format your presentation and make it look beautiful!

These buttons are used to change the alignment of text. Keep it aligned to the left, right, or center.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
Bold

Ctrl+B
Center Align Ctrl+E
Decrease Font Size Ctrl+Shift+<
Increase Font Size Ctrl+Shift+>
Insert Hyperlink Ctrl+K
Insert Picture

Ctrl+P
Italic Ctrl+I
Left Align Ctrl+L
New Slide Ctrl+M
Right Align Ctrl+R
Underline Ctrl+U
